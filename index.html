How to Make Chowmein?

To prepare Chowmein, first boil water in a pan. Add the noodles and cook for a few minutes until soft. Drain the water and keep the noodles aside.

Chowmein Stuff

Open the Chowmein packet and take out the noodles. Boil them for 10 minutes, then drain the water and keep them in a bowl.

Utensils

We need a pan, plate, spoon, and knife to prepare Chowmein. These utensils help us cook and serve the food.

Vegetables

Vegetables like carrot, cabbage, onion, and capsicum are used to make Chowmein tasty and healthy.

Oil

Any cooking oil can be used to prepare delicious Chowmein.
